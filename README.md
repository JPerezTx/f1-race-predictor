# F1 Race Predictor

I'm testing how well simple models predict Formula 1 race results.

## Question
How accurately can qualifying position, plus one or two other factors,
predict the finishing order of a race?

## Data
FastF1 (Python library for official F1 timing and results data).

## Method (planned)
1. Baseline: predict finishing order = qualifying order.
2. Add one factor (recent form or track type).
3. Compare predictions to actual results across 10+ races.

## Limits
Results will cover a small sample of races and won't account for
weather, crashes, or strategy.
